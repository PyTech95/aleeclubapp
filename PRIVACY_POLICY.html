1 Introduction

Alee Club ("we", "us", "our", or "the Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application Alee Club (the "App"), available on Google Play, the Apple App Store, and our website www.aleeclub.net.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with Google Play User Data Policy, Apple App Store Review Guidelines, the Information Technology Act 2000 (India) and applicable data-protection regulations.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal Information you provide

  • Full Name — collected during registration
  • Phone Number — for OTP-based sign-in and event-related communication
  • Email Address — for email/password sign-in and notifications
  • City — to show city-specific pageants and events
  • Age & Date of Birth — to determine pageant-category eligibility
  • Gender — to display gender-appropriate event categories
  • Profile Information — bio, achievements, height, social-media handles you choose to add

2.2 Content you upload

  • Profile photo and cover photo
  • Audition portfolio — photos and videos

2.3 Application & Payment data

  • Events you apply to, application status, judge feedback, certificates issued.
  • Payment reference data (order id, transaction id, amount, payment status) — we do NOT store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or banking credentials. All payments are processed by Razorpay.

2.4 Automatically collected data

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, app version, device identifiers (used only for crash reports and security).
  • Log data — IP address, access times, app interaction events (anonymous, used to improve the App).
  • We do NOT collect precise GPS location.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Account management — create, maintain and secure your account
  • Event participation — process your pageant applications, payments, judging and certificates
  • Communication — send important notifications about events, application status, results and announcements
  • Customer support — respond to your queries and resolve issues
  • Personalization — show events and content relevant to your city, age and category
  • Security & fraud prevention — detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Service improvement — analyze anonymized usage patterns to improve features
  • Legal compliance — comply with applicable laws, regulations and lawful requests from authorities

We do NOT sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4 Account Information

When you sign up for Alee Club we create an account associated with your phone number and/or email address. You can sign in using:

  • Phone OTP — a one-time password sent to your registered phone number
  • Email & Password — with bcrypt-hashed password storage
  • Google Sign-In — via the secure Google OAuth flow

You can update or delete your account information at any time from the Profile section of the App, or by writing to us at aleetrust9@gmail.com.

5 Camera Permission

The App requests Camera access for the following purposes:

  • Capture profile photos for your audition portfolio
  • Record audition videos directly in the App

Camera access is only used when you explicitly tap the camera icon. We never access the camera in the background. You can revoke camera permission at any time through your device's Settings → Apps → Alee Club → Permissions.

6 Photos & Media Access

The App requests Photos / Media Library access (or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+) for:

  • Selecting existing photos from your gallery to upload to your portfolio
  • Selecting existing videos from your gallery to upload as audition clips

Only the media files you specifically select are uploaded. We do NOT scan your entire gallery, and we do NOT access any media without your explicit selection.

7 Storage Permission

On older Android versions (Android 12 and below) the App requests READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission for:

  • Saving your downloaded participation certificates to your device
  • Saving your Alee ID card image when you tap the "Save" button
  • Caching App data for offline access

On Android 13+ this permission is replaced by the scoped READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO permissions described in Section 6.

8 Notifications

We use push notifications and in-app notifications to keep you informed about:

  • Application status updates (submitted, shortlisted, selected, etc.)
  • Upcoming pageant deadlines and event reminders
  • Announcements from organizers
  • Important security or account-related alerts

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings. Doing so will not affect your ability to use the rest of the App, but you may miss time-sensitive updates.

9 Third-Party Services

We share limited information with the following trusted third-party providers, strictly to operate the App:

  • Razorpay — to securely process registration-fee payments. Razorpay handles all card / UPI / netbanking data per its own PCI-DSS-compliant privacy policy.
  • Google (Sign-In) — when you sign in with Google. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube — embedded videos (founder, reality show, achievements) are served by YouTube and subject to YouTube's privacy practices.
  • Anthropic Claude AI — used for AI-powered profile scoring. We send only the public portions of your profile (name, bio, achievements, category) and never any private credentials or payment data.
  • Cloud hosting partners — our servers are hosted on industry-standard cloud infrastructure with end-to-end encryption.

We do NOT share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.

10 Data Security

We take data security seriously and use industry-standard safeguards:

  • Encryption in transit — all data is transmitted over HTTPS / TLS 1.2+
  • Password security — passwords are hashed using bcrypt with per-user salts
  • Authentication — JWT bearer tokens with limited validity
  • Access controls — strict role-based access (participant / judge / admin) on the backend
  • Payment isolation — no card data is ever stored on our servers
  • Regular audits — we review our security posture periodically

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11 Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and above. Many of our pageants are designed for teens and kids.

  • Participants under 18 years of age must register with the consent of a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to be bound by these terms on the child's behalf.
  • We do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at aleetrust9@gmail.com and we will promptly delete the account.

We comply with COPPA (US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) principles to the extent applicable.

12 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — view your data directly in the Profile section of the App
  • Correction — update your name, phone, email, city and profile info at any time
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account by writing to us. We will delete your account within 30 days, except for payment receipts retained for tax compliance.
  • Portability — request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — sign out of the App and uninstall it at any time
  • Object — object to certain processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint — contact your local data-protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights please email us at aleetrust9@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
